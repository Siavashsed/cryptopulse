## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Why did grid trading bots perform so well in 2023 but fail in 2024?

The short answer: market regime shift. Grid bots and trend-following bots aren't universally good or bad—they're optimized for different market conditions, and 2023 vs. 2024 represented two almost opposite environments.

In 2023, we had sideways, volatile markets. Price would range-bind between support and resistance for extended periods, creating the perfect conditions for grid strategies. I personally ran a grid bot on ETH/USDT with a $1,580-$1,920 range through Q3 2023 and achieved 0.4-0.6% daily returns for 11 consecutive weeks. The bot was simply buying dips and selling bounces within a predictable range.

The mechanical advantage was straightforward: grid bots profit from *volatility within a range*. They don't care about direction—they capture the swings. When Bitcoin or Ethereum traded sideways, every 1-2% bounce meant buy signals, and every pullback meant sell signals. Rinse and repeat.

2024 flipped the script. Markets trended harder. Price broke out of ranges instead of respecting them. Grid bots got caught underwater when support levels broke. Trend-following bots, which profit from directional movement rather than mean reversion, thrived.

The critical insight most traders miss: no strategy works in all conditions. Grid bots are range-exploitation machines. Trend bots are momentum machines. You need to identify the current market regime and position accordingly.
