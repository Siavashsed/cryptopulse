## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Which on-chain indicators have actually signaled Bitcoin market tops in the past?

Having spent years as a quant trader building crypto models, I'd point to three on-chain indicators that flagged the last three Bitcoin cycle tops: the MVRV Z-Score, the Puell Multiple, and long-term holder distribution.

The MVRV Z-Score compares market cap to realized cap, which is roughly the aggregate cost basis of all coins. When the market value stretches far above what holders paid, the market is historically overheated. It was extremely loud in 2017.

The Puell Multiple divides daily miner revenue by its 365-day moving average. Spikes show miners earning far above their norm, which has tended to coincide with euphoric prices.

Long-term holder distribution tracks when coins that haven't moved for months or years start being sold. When experienced holders offload into strength, supply is moving from strong hands to new buyers, which is often a late-cycle pattern.

Here's the important caveat: none of these worked alone, and their sensitivity changes over time. The signal that screamed in 2017 was much quieter by 2021, largely because the market grew and the structure of buyers changed. I learned this personally on April 14, 2021, when an MVRV alert fired and I sold a third of my position at $61,400. The top zone read was right, but the exact timing was not, and price kept moving in ways that tested my conviction.

My advice is to use these as a confluence checklist rather than a trigger. When two or three flash together, raise your caution and scale out gradually instead of trying to call the exact peak. Also re-fit your thresholds each cycle rather than trusting old levels blindly.
